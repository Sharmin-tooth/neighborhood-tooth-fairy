Services

Mobile Dental Hygiene Services

A closer look at what each visit can include. Every service is provided in the comfort of your home or care setting, and tailored to your individual needs.

1

Dental Cleaning

Routine preventive cleanings help remove plaque and surface buildup between dental visits. Sessions are scheduled around your comfort and take place wherever you're most at ease — your living room, a care facility, or your bedside.

2

Periodontal Maintenance

For patients with a history of gum disease, ongoing periodontal maintenance helps support gum health between visits with a dentist or periodontist. Sessions focus on the areas that need it most, at a pace that's comfortable for you.

3

Deep Cleaning / Scaling & Root Planing

When appropriate, scaling and root planing reach below the gumline to remove buildup that a routine cleaning can't. This deeper approach is done gently, and only when it's the right fit for your needs.

4

Fluoride Treatment

A simple fluoride application can help strengthen enamel and support prevention against decay — especially valuable for patients who may have difficulty keeping up with daily brushing and flossing on their own.

5

Desensitizing Treatment

For patients dealing with tooth sensitivity, desensitizing treatments can help ease discomfort during eating, drinking, or daily hygiene, making everyday comfort easier to maintain.

6

Oral Cancer Screening

A simple visual and tactile examination of the mouth, lips, and surrounding tissue can help identify areas that may need a closer look. Any areas of concern are noted, and a referral is made for further evaluation when appropriate.

7

Oral Camera Images

An intraoral camera lets you see what's happening inside your own mouth. These images help document changes over time and make it easier to communicate clearly with you, your family, or a referring dentist.

8

Oral Hygiene Education

Every visit includes personalized guidance on brushing, flossing, and the right hygiene products for your specific needs — practical advice that caregivers and family members can help carry forward between visits.

9

Nutritional Guidance for Oral Health

Diet plays a real role in oral health. Sessions can include education about the dietary habits that affect tooth decay and gum health, so you have practical information to support your overall oral wellness.

10

Referrals

Mobile dental hygiene care is not a replacement for a dentist. When something requires diagnosis or treatment beyond hygiene services, an appropriate referral is made to a dentist or another healthcare professional.
