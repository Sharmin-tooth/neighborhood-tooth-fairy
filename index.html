Services

Mobile Dental Hygiene Services

We provide professional preventive dental hygiene services in the comfort of your home or care setting.

Dental Cleaning

Preventive dental hygiene care provided in your home or residential setting.

Periodontal Maintenance

Ongoing care for patients with a history of gum disease to support gum health.

Deep Cleaning / Scaling & Root Planing

Deeper cleaning below the gumline when appropriate to help improve periodontal health.

Fluoride Treatment

Fluoride applications to strengthen teeth and help prevent decay.

Desensitizing Treatment

Options to help reduce tooth sensitivity and improve comfort.

Oral Cancer Screening

A visual and tactile examination of the mouth to identify areas that may require further evaluation or referral.

Oral Camera Images

Intraoral images may be taken to help document and explain areas of concern and assist with communication and referrals.

Oral Hygiene Education

Personalized recommendations for brushing, flossing, oral hygiene products, and daily home care.

Nutritional Guidance for Oral Health

Education about dietary habits that may affect tooth decay, gum health, and overall oral wellness.

Referrals

When dental conditions require diagnosis or treatment beyond mobile dental hygiene services, appropriate referrals can be made to dentists and other healthcare professionals.
